About Mobola

The story, mission, and vision behind Abeokuta's trusted physiotherapy and fitness center.

The Founder

Meet Mobola

Founded with a vision to bring world-class physiotherapy and fitness services to Abeokuta, Mobola has grown into a trusted health destination.

With extensive training in musculoskeletal physiotherapy, sports rehabilitation, and therapeutic massage, our team has successfully treated hundreds of patients.

We believe in treating the whole person, not just the symptoms. Every treatment plan is personalized, every session is meaningful, and every patient is family.
